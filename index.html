Our Story

Rooted in Veda,
Rooted in Care

The word Vedika means a sacred platform of knowledge. Our centre was founded on one belief — true health is more than the absence of disease. It is swasthya: harmony of body, mind and soul.

Our vaidyas, yoga gurus and therapists combine classical Ayurvedic texts with gentle modern understanding, creating a plan that is unique to your prakriti (nature).

  • Traditional herbal, preservative-free formulations
  • Personalised dosha-based treatment plans
  • Calm, clean, temple-inspired healing spaces
