Our Story

Food is the first medicine

Vedika Wellness began with a simple idea: good health starts on your plate. Our team of nutritionists, doctors and yoga coaches helps you build habits that last — using the everyday Indian foods you already love.

We combine modern nutrition science with traditional wisdom like millets, turmeric, ghee in balance and mindful eating, so your plan feels natural, not forced.

  • Evidence-based, personalised plans
  • No extreme diets or expensive supplements
  • Friendly support on WhatsApp
